## Important image rules

- Use real photos only.
- Do not use random internet images.
- Avoid screenshots if possible.
- Prefer horizontal photos for hero and experience sections.
- Prefer clean, bright, high-resolution images.
- The hero image must be the strongest ocean-view photo.
- Room photos should look clean and inviting.
- Keep filenames exactly as written above.
- After adding images, the developer will wire them into the components.
